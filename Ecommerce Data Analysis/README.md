# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Behaviour Analysis

Exploratory analysis of online retail transactions in Python, examining sales
trends, product performance, geographic concentration and return behaviour.

## Key findings
- Sales show a clear upward trend over time with visible seasonal fluctuations.
- A small number of products drive a disproportionate share of revenue and
  volume — key inventory drivers.
- Revenue is concentrated in a limited number of countries, indicating strong
  core markets and scope for targeted regional expansion.
- Returns are unevenly distributed, concentrated in a small subset of products.

## Recommendations
- Prioritise inventory and promotion around top-performing products.
- Review pricing and quality for high-return products to cut return costs.
- Focus resources on high-revenue countries while exploring emerging markets.

## Tools
Python, pandas, matplotlib, seaborn. Paired with a Power BI dashboard.
