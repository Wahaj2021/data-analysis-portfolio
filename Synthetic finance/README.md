# Financial Transactions Analysis (Synthetic Data)

Exploratory analysis of synthetic personal-finance data in Python, examining
how income, loans, savings and credit behaviour relate across customer segments.

## Questions explored
- How do income and spending differ between loan holders and non-holders?
- Do savings levels vary by region?
- How does employment status affect income and expenses?
- Is credit score related to loan holding?

## Key findings
- Loan holders show distinct income and spending patterns, suggesting loan
  products are associated with specific income segments.
- Savings levels vary by region, indicating geographic differences in financial
  capacity.
- Employment status affects both income and expenses.

## Tools
Python, pandas, matplotlib, seaborn. Paired with a Power BI dashboard.
