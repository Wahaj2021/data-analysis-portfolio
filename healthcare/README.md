# Healthcare Insurance Cost Analysis

Exploratory analysis of medical insurance data in Python to identify what
drives healthcare charges.

## Key findings
- Charges are highly concentrated — a small group of high-cost individuals
  drives a large share of total spending.
- **Smoking is the strongest and most consistent driver** of higher charges,
  across age groups, BMI levels, regions and family sizes.
- Charges rise with age, reflecting greater expected healthcare needs.
- BMI shows a positive relationship with charges, especially combined with
  other risk factors.

## Recommendations
- Factor smoking status heavily into risk and pricing models.
- Target cost-management and wellness programmes at high-risk, high-cost groups.

## Tools
Python, pandas, matplotlib, seaborn. Paired with a Power BI dashboard.
